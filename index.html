Unsaid support

Unsaid helps you explore a chat export on your phone. If something is not working, email suryadeep@decafdevs.com.

Before you write

  • To cancel or change a subscription, open your Apple App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
  • To restore a purchase, use Restore purchases on Unsaid’s subscription screen.
  • To delete an imported chat, use Unsaid’s Privacy & storage settings.

Please describe the device, app version, and what happened. Do not email your chat export or private messages.

For billing-data deletion requests, write to the same address. We may need a store transaction identifier to locate a purchase; please do not send payment card details.
